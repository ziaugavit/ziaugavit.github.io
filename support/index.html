Common questions

How do I aim?
Hold your phone up like a pistol and point it downrange. The green dot on the front sight is where the bullet goes. Tap anywhere to fire, and use the button at the bottom right to reload. If the aim drifts, open the pause menu and choose Recenter aim.

I bought something on another device or reinstalled the game.
Open the shop or the Remove Ads screen and tap Restore purchases. Remove Ads and the Sharpshooter Pack come back automatically. Power-up bundles are used up as you play, so they can't be restored.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Why do I need stars to open a new map?
Each mission unlocks the next one once you earn at least one star. A new map opens when you've collected enough stars on the map before it.

Ads
Short ads may show between some missions. You can also choose to watch an ad for a free power-up. Buying Remove Ads or the Sharpshooter Pack turns off the ads between missions.
